## Start-here findings

**Dashboard clipping/scrolling:** not reproduced in this checkout and session. At 375×812, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth = 375`; the settled dashboard document height was **829px**. The document/root had `overflow: visible`, and the scan found no vertically overflowing `auto/scroll` descendant. No measured element extended outside the viewport on this default dashboard. The screenshot showed the card's right border. A page scrollbar is expected for an 829px document in an 812px viewport. **The cause of the previously reported missing right border remains unknown.** Reproduce on the affected iPhone with its exact URL, content, browser zoom and scroll position before changing overflow rules; do not hide overflow as a guessed fix.

**Phone assistant scroll lock:** `ca1108b` adds `html.style.overflow = hidden` and `overscrollBehavior = none` while the phone chat is mounted, with cleanup on unmount (`features/assistant/components/assistant-chat.tsx:57`). The lock is present in the phone DOM and normal page scrolling returns after navigation. Native Safari bounce is still a hardware check. Recent remains inside the same locked app view.

**Small controls:** Chat/Recent and dashboard tabs measured **27px** high. Their underline pseudo-element is not a 44px hit-area enlargement. The normal Button component's `pointer-coarse:after:min-h-11/min-w-11` was explicitly accounted for: 32–40px visual buttons carrying that helper were **not** reported merely for their visual size.

## Contrast measurements

Computed colors in this browser are CSS `lab()`, not plain RGB. Measurements converted Lab D50 through XYZ/adaptation to sRGB, composited solid ancestor backgrounds and calculated WCAG luminance ratios. Transparent, image, moving-gradient, filter and opacity compositions are not globally certified by this method. Initial simple color scans were discarded where unsupported color syntax prevented a calculation.

| Sample | Light ratio | Dark ratio | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Foreground body/heading text on white | 19.79:1 | Not a blanket dark-theme measurement | Pass for sampled light text |
| Muted 14px public category text on white | 4.73:1 | Not separately recorded | Pass, close to 4.5:1 threshold |
| Team initials on the shared muted avatar fallback, 14px | **4.34:1** | **5.86:1** | **Light fails 4.5:1; dark sample passes** |

The avatar failure was measured on the public team list and booking professional step, in English and Spanish. Root cause: `text-muted-foreground` over `bg-muted` in `components/ui/avatar.tsx:48`. Avoid treating all muted-on-white text as failing; the background pairing is material.

Photo-count overlays use white text over `bg-black/40` and an image (`features/business-page/components/business-photos.tsx:231`). Contrast depends on the image pixel and was **not verified**; a flat-background calculation would be invalid. Likewise, the home highlighter draws an SVG behind text; ignoring that SVG produces a false failure. Neither was counted as a measured contrast violation.
